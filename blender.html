01 — WHAT IS BLENDER?

A digital space
to create in 3D.

Blender is a free, open-source 3D creation tool.

It gives artists, designers, students and creators a place to build objects and worlds on a computer. A model can be simple and playful, or detailed and realistic. Once a scene is built, it can be given materials, lit, viewed through a camera and turned into a final image or animation.

Think of it as a digital workshop: instead of working with physical clay, wood or paint, we work with shapes, surfaces, light and virtual cameras.
