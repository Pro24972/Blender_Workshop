01 — THE BIG IDEA

What if you could
build an idea?

That is the exciting possibility of 3D design.

In our Blender workshop, we explored a digital space where imagination meets practical skills. We learned how a simple starting shape can be developed into something recognisable, detailed and ready to present.

This website shares what Blender can do, what we explored as a team, and two projects from our workshop.

02 — WHY 3D?

More than making
things look real.

3D design brings together creativity and careful thinking. It asks us to imagine an object, plan how it is built, make choices about its appearance, and refine the result.
